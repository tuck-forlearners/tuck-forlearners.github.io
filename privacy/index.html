Tuck for Learners: Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

The short version: Tuck saves the homework questions you answer so you can review them later. Everything it saves stays in your own browser. Tuck has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it never sends your data anywhere.

What Tuck saves

When you answer a question on a supported homework site (currently McGraw Hill SmartBook and Norton InQuizitive), Tuck saves:

  • the question text, its answer choices, the correct answer and any explanations shown on the page;
  • the answer you chose, whether it was right, the date, and the confidence level you selected where the site asks for one;
  • the course and chapter name shown on the site, or one you typed in yourself;
  • your practice history inside Tuck: quiz answers, practice exam results and any explanations you type in "Explain why" mode;
  • your settings, such as your theme and last-used filters.

Tuck only saves questions after you have answered them. It never reads your login details, grades or other parts of your account, and it never fills in or selects answers for you.

Where it is stored

All of this is stored locally in your browser, using Chrome's built-in storage on your own device. It is not uploaded, synced or backed up by Tuck. The developer of Tuck cannot see it.

What Tuck does not do

  • Tuck does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or student ID.
  • Tuck does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
  • Tuck does not sell, rent, share or transfer user data, and does not use it for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than showing your saved questions back to you.
  • Tuck does not use cookies, analytics, tracking or remote code.

Permissions

  • Storage: to save your questions and settings in your browser.
  • Access to learning.mheducation.com, sso.router.integration.prod.mheducation.com and ncia.wwnorton.com: to read the questions you answer on SmartBook and InQuizitive, and the course and chapter names shown on SmartBook's assignment start page and in InQuizitive. Tuck does not run on any other website.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: the dashboard's menu has "Export CSV", which downloads your saved questions as a spreadsheet file.
  • Delete part of it: you can delete a single course or chapter from the Question bank.
  • Delete everything: use "Delete all data" in the dashboard's menu, or uninstall Tuck. Uninstalling removes all data Tuck stored.

Feedback

Tuck links to two short feedback forms, both hosted by Google Forms:

  • Send feedback: a link in Tuck's popup and in the dashboard's menu opens the feedback form in a new tab.
  • Uninstall form: if you remove Tuck, Chrome opens a short form asking why.

Both forms are optional; you can close them without answering. They only collect what you type or select in them. Tuck adds nothing to the form links, so none of your saved questions, answers, course names or other data is sent with them. Google's privacy policy applies to the forms.

Children

Tuck is designed for high school, college and adult learners. Because it collects no personal information and sends nothing off your device, it does not collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If Tuck's handling of data ever changes, for example if an optional feature that sends data off your device is added, this page will be updated before that change is released, and any such feature will be off unless you choose to turn it on.
