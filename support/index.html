Frequently asked questions

Tuck isn't saving my questions

  • Refresh your homework tab after installing or updating Tuck. Tabs that were already open before can't save until they are refreshed.
  • Tuck works only on McGraw Hill SmartBook and Norton InQuizitive.
  • Open SmartBook assignments from their start page, so Tuck knows the course and chapter.

Where is my data?

Only in your browser, on your own device. Tuck has no servers and no accounts. Uninstalling Tuck deletes everything it saved, so export a CSV first if you want to keep a copy (dashboard → Question bank → ⋯ menu → Export CSV).

How do I delete a course or start over?

Open the dashboard, go to the Question bank and open the ⋯ menu. Choose "Delete a course…" to remove one course, or "Delete all data…" to start over.

Which sites does Tuck work with?

McGraw Hill SmartBook and Norton InQuizitive, for now. If you'd like Tuck to work with another homework site, suggest it through the feedback form.
