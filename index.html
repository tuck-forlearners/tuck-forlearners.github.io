How it works

Do your homework. Tuck does the remembering.

No copying questions into flashcards, no screenshots, no new habits. Just study better when it counts.

1

Answer as usual

Work through your SmartBook or InQuizitive assignment like always. Tuck runs quietly in the background.

2

Tuck saves it

After you answer, Tuck saves the question, the right answer, your answer and the explanation, sorted by course and chapter.

3

Study what you missed

Before the exam, press Study now. Tuck starts with your misses and marks questions mastered as you get them right.
